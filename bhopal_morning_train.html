Bhopal Morning Train

Gwalior (GWL) → Bhopal: every train from Sun 15 Nov 00:00 to Mon 16 Nov 12:00

Plan: leave GWL on Sunday evening and reach Bhopal by Mon 16 Nov 08:00.

Sources:

  • Train timings: tr.pdf (Sun 15 Nov) and tr2.pdf (Mon 16 Nov).
  • Chart times: IRCTC reservation-chart screenshots of the 14 Nov trains.

How to read the chart columns:

  • 1st chart: the clock time the 1st chart came out (seen on the 14 Nov train).
  • Gap before charting stn: how long before the train reaches its charting station the 1st chart came out.
    • For trains that start at the charting station (NZM, NDLS), the departure time is used.
    • For trains charted at GWL, the GWL departure time is used.
  • Charting-station times at AGC, NZM and NDLS come from public timetable sites (railmitra, ixigo, travelkhana). Confirm them on NTES.
  • The same train usually gets its chart at about the same clock time each day.
  • ? = not yet collected.

Arrival station is Bhopal Jn (BPL) unless marked RKMP (Rani Kamalapati) or SHRN (Sant Hirdaram Nagar).

Notes

  • The "Journey Date" on a chart is the date the train leaves the boarding station (GWL).
  • Charts come out about 7½–8 hours before departure for trains charted at their starting station (20806, 12780). For trains charted partway along the route, it's about 9–10 hours before they reach the charting station (12626, 12156, 22692, 12616, 12724, 12191, 12406, 16032, 22222, 12628, 12622).
  • After charting, none of 12626, 12156, 12628 or 12622 sell GWL→BPL tickets online. The only way is the TTE on board. Check the "TTE can book till / Online booking" note on every other chart: that note decides which trains you can actually book online.
  • To fill the ? cells, open the chart for journey date 14/11 with boarding station GWL. Alternatively, run console_charts.js in the browser console on irctc.co.in/online-charts.
